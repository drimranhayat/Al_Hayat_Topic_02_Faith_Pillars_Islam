# Repository 2: final learning coverage audit

40 hadith selections · 105 explanatory Q&A · 138 MCQs · 145 mapped teaching points

Coverage of the forty selected published texts and their main explanatory distinctions. All source benefits and available vocabulary remain readable in their panels. Incidental descriptive details do not each require an isolated question. This is an editorial coverage audit, not independent scholarly certification or exhaustive coverage of every chain, variant or legal debate.

Every question has an exact publisher-source anchor and a visible supporting passage. A source anchor verifies the cited wording, not every interpretive claim by itself. Teaching-point mappings reflect editorial review. Source texts, report IDs, existing question IDs, notes, bookmarks and reading-progress keys are retained. Quiz attempts use a new revision key because the practice bank has changed.
