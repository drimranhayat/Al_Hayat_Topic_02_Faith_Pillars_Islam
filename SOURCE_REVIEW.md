# Topic 002 — Source and content review

Faith and the Pillars of Islam. Prepared 17 October 2026.

## Selection standard

Forty distinct publisher records, each labelled Authentic by HadeethEnc, with Arabic text, English and Urdu translations and explanations. Each selected narration has a reference in Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim. Alternate source IDs for the same narration, such as the duplicate five-pillar and branches-of-faith records, were not counted twice. This is publisher-based selection, not a fresh independent grading of forty chains.

There is no source-ID overlap with the completed Topic 001 collection. Both topics may naturally share themes or familiar phrases. Reports on similar subjects, such as Talhah's Najdi questioner and Anas's account of Dimam, are distinct accounts rather than duplicated chains of one entry.

## Sources retained

Eighty original API responses are included, one English and one Urdu record per selected ID. The English responses contain the displayed Arabic, English, Arabic explanation, and Arabic glossary. The Urdu responses supply Urdu translations, explanations and benefits. `content-manifest.json` records file checksums. Published fields are not silently rewritten.

Publisher page metadata was retrieved for all forty selected records and retained in `reference-provenance.json`. It includes the specific reference where supplied by the publisher. Primary collection links were checked; suffixes were corrected where a base number did not resolve. Targeted full-text cross-checks were used for contextual and attribution questions. Wordings and numbering can vary between source versions.

## Selection and context issues addressed

- Candidate 66271 had no available Urdu API response. It was replaced by authenticated record 8883, a related but distinct Abu Dharr report on unfounded accusations, referenced to Bukhari 6045. No translation was invented.
- Record 65018 is attributed by the publisher to Muslim and Ahmad, with its page naming Musnad Ahmad 15416. The site's primary link is the corresponding Sufyan report in Muslim 38. This is explicitly identified in the source panel.
- Record 3055 preserves the publisher explanation unchanged. Its explanation mentions an occasion found in a separate Ibn Umar report, Bukhari 24. The selected Imran wording appears in Bukhari 6117 with a different surrounding exchange. An editorial context note makes the distinction visible; the questions use the definition of modesty, not the disputed occasion.
- English and Urdu API records for 3284 and 3599 use different Arabic presentation, introductions, and small wording/conjunction variations for the same core report. Both source responses are retained. The displayed Arabic follows the English record; the Urdu translation follows its own publisher response. These are not treated as identical serialized texts.
- Practical hypocrisy is distinguished from creedal hypocrisy using the publisher's explicit explanation of record 65124.
- The explanation of record 65002 qualifies the contrasting case through hypocrisy or renouncing Islam. The questions preserve that qualification rather than claiming that every later lapse restores all pre-Islamic sins.
- Record 65001 is taught with the source's safeguards of knowledge, ability, and avoiding greater evil.
- The promises concerning regular worship, fasting, Hajj and purification keep the qualifications given by their sources. Unwanted thoughts are distinguished from willing acceptance in records 65011 and 65013.

## Teaching standard

105 explanatory answers and 138 MCQs in the final coverage edition. Each has an exact checked source anchor. Exact matching establishes traceability; it does not independently prove an interpretation. Counts vary with the content: one to twelve answers and one to fourteen MCQs. Every retained item develops a specific point. Generic narrator, reference and sanad questions are not repeated throughout the bank.

All original benefits and glossary entries remain available separately in the reading panels. Incorrect quiz choices are explicitly editorial exercise distractors, not alleged hadith. Full chains are linked through primary collections and are not reconstructed.

## Verification and limits

Checks cover source-field preservation, forty distinct records, five sections of eight, source anchors for all ninety-five learning items, key/answer consistency, unique stems, balanced answer positions, and all 188 answer-choice paths. Interaction checks include search, filters, direct selection, bookmarks, progress, notes, routes, quiz reset and saved reload. HTML structure, collapsed panels, local links and reading color contrast are checked.

Browser visual QA was unavailable in this environment; structural and simulated interaction checks were completed. No independent specialist assessment or exhaustive scholarly coverage is claimed. Use the linked full sources and appropriate scholarship for disputed applications or individual rulings.

The final per-report coverage audit is supplied in `COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`, `coverage-audit.csv` and `coverage-audit.json`. It records 145 mapped teaching points. See `CONTENT_REVIEW.md` for the detailed editorial and technical review.
