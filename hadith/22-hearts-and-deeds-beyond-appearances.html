22

Hearts and deeds, beyond appearances

The heart and the strength of faith
Sahih
Arabic · Published text

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنَّ اللهَ لَا يَنْظُرُ إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ وَأَمْوَالِكُمْ، وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ».

English · Translation

Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Indeed, Allah does not look at your appearance and wealth, but He looks at your hearts and deeds.”

Urdu · Translation

ابو ہریرہ رضی اللہ عنہ سے روایت ہے، وہ کہتے ہیں کہ اللہ کے رسول ﷺ نے فرمایا: ”اللہ تمھارے جسموں اور تمھاری شکلوں کو نہیں دیکھتا، بلکہ وہ تمھارے دلوں اور اعمال کو دیکھتا ہے“۔

Sahih Muslim 2564c ↗Source attribution: Narrated by Muslim
English explanation+
The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) points out that Allah Almighty does not look at the appearance and bodies of His servants, as to whether they are beautiful or ugly, big or small, and sound or sick. And He does not look at their wealth as to whether they possess much or little. Indeed, Allah Almighty does not punish His servants or hold them accountable for these matters and their different levels regarding them. But He looks at their hearts and the piety, certitude, truthfulness, and sincerity existing therein or their intention to show off and attain a good reputation among people. He looks at their deeds and sees whether they are righteous or evil, and He gives the recompense accordingly.
Urdu explanation+
اللہ کے نبی صلی اللہ علیہ و سلم بتا رہے ہیں کہ اللہ بندوں کے رنگ وروپ اور ان کے کی جسموں کو نہیں دیکھتا کہ خوب صورت ہيں یا بد صورت؟ تندرست ہیں یا بیمار؟ ان کی دولت بھی نہیں دیکھتا کہ کم ہے یا زیادہ؟ اللہ عز و جل ان چیزوں اور ان چیزوں میں ان کے تفاوت کی بنیاد پر ان کا حساب نہیں لیتا۔ اللہ ان کے دلوں اور دلوں کے اندر موجود تقوی، یقین، سچائی، اخلاص یا پھر ریاکاری اور شہرت طلبی کو دیکھتا ہے۔ اللہ بندوں کے اعمال کو دیکھتا ہے کہ صحیح ہیں یا غلط اور انھیں باتوں کی بنیاد پر ان کو جزا یا سزا دیتا ہے۔

Lessons and benefits+
  • We should be careful to mend our hearts and purify them of any bad traits.
  • Hearts become sound with sincerity, and deeds become sound by following the Prophet's example. It is both of them that Allah Almighty looks at and judges accordingly.
  • A person should not be deceived by wealth, beauty, body, or worldly appearances.
  • The Hadīth warns us against caring about outward appearance and failing to reform what is inside.

Meaning and understanding

01Why does the report mention hearts and deeds together?
The explanation locates accountability in the heart's sincerity, piety and intention, and in whether deeds are righteous or evil. Wealth, beauty and bodily condition are not substitutes. Mentioning the heart does not make conduct irrelevant, and visible activity does not remove the need to examine intention.
Check the supporting passage

02How do the benefits connect reform of the heart with reform of deeds?
They connect sincerity with a sound heart and following the Prophet's example with sound deeds. Concern for the inner life and concern for outward obedience are complementary, rather than alternatives.
Check the supporting passage
